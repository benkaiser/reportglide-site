Fictional writing example

Keep the context in the wording.

Supplied note

5/10 requests with visual choice board + verbal cue in clinic. Parent says not using board at home yet.

Report wording

In the clinic, 5 of 10 requests were made using a visual choice board and a verbal cue. The parent reports that the board is not yet being used at home.

Illustrative wording, not a clinical recommendation or a validated product output. It preserves the support provided and does not imply the skill generalises to home.
