Australian data sovereignty

Australian storage isn’t
the whole story.

A report can be saved on an Australian server while its contents are sent overseas for AI processing. Ask about both.

Australian data boundary
Supplied notes & files→AI processing→Reports & backups

ReportGlide stores and processes patient content in Australia by default, using Australian-region AI inference. No overseas model fallback.

Residency, not a badge.

We mean where the data is stored and where the model processes it. This does not mean every infrastructure provider is Australian-owned, or that geography alone establishes privacy compliance.

The whole boundary

Inference, files, databases, backups, logs, monitoring and support access all stay in Australia. We can share the service’s provider and data-handling details on request.

Inference runs on Amazon Bedrock with routing restricted to Australian regions.

Questions worth asking any provider.

Where does the text go?

Check inference regions, document extraction, redaction services and fallback routes, not just the final report’s storage location.

What else is retained?

Ask about logs, backups, deletion, support access and whether provider contracts allow content to be used for model training.

What is documented?

Ask for current details of the service configuration and subprocessors. “Australian hosted” is not a compliance certification.
