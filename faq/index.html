Frequently asked questions

A few practical answers.

Does it make clinical decisions?

No. You supply the findings, interpretation and recommendations. The purpose is to organise and refine that information, not diagnose, interpret scores or recommend treatment. You review and approve every report.

Can we use our own Word template?

Yes. We work with your practice’s existing template, including your branding and report structure. If you need a template, we can help with that too.

What happens when something is missing?

The draft should ask you to clarify a gap or contradiction, not fill it with an invented finding. Supplied scores, dates, source attribution and uncertainty need to stay intact.

Does Australian hosting mean Australian AI processing?

Not necessarily. A service can store reports in Australia while sending text to an overseas model. ReportGlide keeps both in Australia. See the distinction and provider comparisons.

How many reports does the monthly allowance cover?

Our estimate is roughly 10–20 OT FCAs or 10–25 speech reassessment reports per person per month. Your practice can share the included usage across the team.

These are estimates from our testing, not fixed report quotas. The speech estimate is based on a communication-focused plan reassessment report, not every type of speech report.

Report length, the amount of source material and revisions affect usage.

How does metered usage work?

Your monthly fee includes a usage allowance. Longer reports and extra revisions use more of it. Additional usage is metered; we’ll confirm how usage is measured and the additional charges before you sign up.
